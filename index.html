Privacy Policy — Supermarket Tycoon

Last updated: 5 October 2026 · applies to version 1.0 and later on iOS and Android

In short: Supermarket Tycoon does not collect any personal data. The game works fully offline, has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no in-app purchases, and does not send your gameplay or any identifier to us or to third parties.

1. Who we are

Supermarket Tycoon is a single-player game published under the 4ES brand (“we”, “us”).

2. Information stored on your device

Your progress (stores, cash, upgrades, staff, settings such as sound, vibration and language, daily rewards and quest state) is saved only in the app’s private storage on your device. We cannot see it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it permanently. Your operating system’s own backup features may include this file according to your device settings.

3. Information we collect

None. The game does not ask for or collect your name, e-mail address, contacts, location, photos, advertising identifier, or any other personal information, and it does not use tracking technologies.

4. Third parties

The game contains no third-party advertising, analytics or attribution SDKs. Downloads and updates are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play under their own privacy policies.

5. Bonuses

Optional in-game bonuses are free and are granted directly by the game; no video advertisements are shown.

6. Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no personal information, we do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Changes

If a future version changes how the game handles data (for example by adding advertising), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.

8. Contact
